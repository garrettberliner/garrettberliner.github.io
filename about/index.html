About Me

I grew up across Puerto Rico, Texas, Virginia, Michigan, New York, Washington, D.C., & Oregon, which taught me to value different perspectives and sparked my interest in technology. I later spent four years conducting computing education research at Oregon State University, contributing to four peer-reviewed publications and two theses. Today, I work as a Technical Program Manager at Microsoft. Outside of work, I enjoy traveling, cooking, playing soccer, hosting board-game nights, & hiking in the Pacific Northwest.
