About

Here's a bit about myself :)!

I grew up moving around the United States - Puerto Rico, Texas, Virginia, Michigan, New York, Washington D.C., & Oregon - which showed me first-hand the immense value & importance that diverse perspectives provide 🌎. During that time, the only way I could keep in touch with friends was through technology & the internet, which sparked my initial curiosity for computer science.

During my undergraduate & master's, I spent over 4 years conducting computing education research under Dr. Jennifer Parham-Mocello. We investigated innovative practices to make CS more accessible & engaging through game-oriented curriculum, along with how to most effectively support & empower K-12 CS teachers without a background in CS. This netted 4 double-blind peer-reviewed publications & 2 theses.

In my free time, I love travelling & experiencing new things! I'm extremely fortunate to live in the Pacific Northwest & take the opportunity to immerse myself in nature whenever I can. A life-goal of mine is visiting all 7 continents ✈️ & all 50 states. Recently, I've been enjoying cooking, playing soccer, hosting board game nights with friends, & hiking 🌲.
